## Full description (max 4,000)
Copy a table from a spreadsheet, a list of numbers from a chat, or a CSV file. Graphro turns it into a chart you can put straight into a presentation. Everything happens on your phone, and the app never makes up a number.

■ Copy, and it's a chart
· Reads tables copied from Excel, Google Sheets or the web, plus CSV, Excel (.xlsx) and JSON files
· Turns loose lines like “Jan revenue $320K, Feb $280,000” into a table
· Share text or a spreadsheet to Graphro from any other app

■ Messy tables, cleaned up for you
· Mixed units like $320K, $280,000 and 1.2M are brought to one scale
· Understands negative markers like (5), percentages, and periods such as Q1 2024, FY2024 and Mar 2024
· Finds and leaves out total rows, including unlabeled rows that equal the sum of the others
· Separates title, unit and source lines, merges two-row headers and fills merged cells
· Flips tables whose years run across the top
· Every cell the app changed is marked, with the original text and the reason. One tap puts it back

■ Tells you which chart fits, and why
· Line, bar, stacked bar, horizontal bar, area and donut charts
· Recommends based on the shape of your data, and explains when a chart doesn't fit

■ Tap to highlight, captions written for you
· Tap a bar or point to highlight it. The caption updates: “Mar revenue rose 46% from the previous month.”
· Automatic highlight of the maximum or biggest change, target line, average line and notes
· A calm style: grey bars with a single highlight color

■ Catches misleading charts (Honesty check)
· Bar axes that don't start at zero: “The largest value is 1.6× the smallest, but the bars make it look 6.7×”
· Donuts with too many slices, shares that don't add up to 100%
· Different units on one axis, unordered items joined by a line
· Careful wording: short periods are never called a “trend”
· Fix each problem with one tap

■ Share as an image, PowerPoint or Excel
· Portrait (4:5) and landscape images for chats and social media
· PowerPoint: a real, editable chart, not a picture, with speaker notes written for you
· Excel: the cleaned table plus a list of every fix

■ Your data never leaves your phone
· No internet permission. No server, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking
· Safe for work, school and government data you can't upload to online AI tools
